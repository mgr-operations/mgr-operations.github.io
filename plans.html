Pricing FAQ

Common questions.

Are these fixed prices?

They are starting points for each scope. After the free scoping call you receive a fixed quote with the deliverables, timeline and price agreed before any work begins.

Is the ClickUp subscription included?

No. ClickUp licenses are purchased directly from ClickUp and are separate from our implementation fees. We can help you choose the right plan for your team size.

How long does an implementation take?

Starter is typically 1–2 weeks, Professional around 4–6 weeks, and Enterprise rollouts are scheduled per phase — usually 8–12 weeks depending on migration and integrations.

We already use ClickUp. Can you still help?

Yes. Most of our work starts in an existing workspace. We audit the current structure, identify what's slowing the team down, and refactor it — without losing your data or history.

How do payments work?

Payment terms are agreed per project before work starts and are included in your quote. Retainers are billed monthly and can be adjusted as your needs change.

Can we start small and upgrade later?

Absolutely. Many teams start with Starter or a single workshop, then move to Professional once the process proves itself. Work done in the Starter package carries over.

Do you support us after launch?

Every package includes post-launch support (14–30+ days), and any engagement can continue on a monthly retainer for ongoing optimization. See Support.
