Support FAQ

Before you write in.

How quickly will you respond?

We aim to reply within one business day. Existing clients on a retainer get priority handling. If it's urgent, mention it in the subject of your request or book a call directly.

I'm having a ClickUp product problem — should I contact you or ClickUp?

For billing, account access, outages or product bugs, contact ClickUp's own support directly. For anything about how your workspace is configured — structure, automations, permissions, dashboards — contact us, since that's the work we build.

Can you help if we haven't started a project yet?

Yes. Pre-project questions are welcome, and a free consultation is the fastest way to get an answer in the context of your actual process. See Plans if you'd like to know pricing first.

Do you offer support without an implementation package?

Yes — monthly retainers cover fixes, improvements and questions for teams that already have a workspace. Training sessions and 1:1 coaching are also available on their own.

What happens after I submit the form?

You'll get a confirmation on screen, then a personal reply from our team — not an automated ticket — with either answers, clarifying questions, or a link to book a call.
